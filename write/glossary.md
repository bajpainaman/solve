# Glossary

**Active voice** - A construction in which the subject performs the action.

**Advisory finding** - A heuristic signal that requires judgment before revision.

**AI-prose overlay** - Non-STE rules that remove model-favored filler, hype,
repetition, and canned structure.

**Approved meaning** - The dictionary meaning for which an approved word can be
used.

**Approved word** - A word shown as permitted in the ASD-STE100 dictionary for a
specified part of speech and meaning.

**Caution** - A safety instruction for a risk of damage to an object.

**Controlled natural language** - A subset of a natural language with restricted
vocabulary and writing rules.

**Descriptive writing** - Text that explains a system, state, behavior, or result.

**Help** - Dictionary guidance that restricts a word, recommends a construction,
or gives other usage information.

**Multi-word noun** - Two or more words that together name one item or concept.

**Nominalization** - A noun form that hides an action, such as `implementation`
instead of `implement`.

**Part of speech** - The grammatical role for which the dictionary approves a word.

**Phrasal verb** - A verb plus another word that creates a meaning not clear from
the verb alone.

**Procedural writing** - Commands and supporting information that tell a reader
how to complete a task.

**Readiness score** - A style-quality indicator from this skill. It is not a
compliance certification.

**Semantic invariant** - Information that a rewrite must preserve, including facts,
modality, conditions, sequence, uncertainty, and literals.

**Technical noun** - A domain term that names an item or concept and meets the
standard's conditions for technical terminology.

**Technical verb** - A domain-specific action term that meets the standard's
conditions for technical verbs.

**Warning** - A safety instruction for a risk of injury or death.
