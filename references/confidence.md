# Confidence follows evidence

Do not convert framework count, agreement, hat votes, or completed artifacts into
a confidence score. Do not generate a Bayesian posterior from judgmental likelihood
ratios. Tiers do not cap or increase certainty.

The recommendation must identify:

- The critical claims and their acceptance conditions.
- The observations supporting or refuting those claims.
- Independent refutation findings, including omissions from the claim ledger.
- Remaining unknowns and the consequence of each being wrong.
- Forecast assumptions and when the outcome will be checked.
- The action the user can take, its reversal cost, and its stop condition.

A completed gate can report a failure. A complete ledger can still fail to justify
a recommendation. An unverified claim cannot be promoted by agreement between
agents. Coverage assessments must examine what the builder left out.

Use bin/gates validate as the mechanical source for current receipt validity.
The refuter checks independent evidence. The consumer checks the delivered report
and declared surfaces. Neither guarantees future strategic success.

If uncertainty can be reduced by a safe, informative experiment, name the experiment
and its observable success/failure criteria. If a commitment depends on missing
evidence, say what remains unverified and which action must wait. Do not convert a
user value choice into an allegedly factual ranking.

Only compute a numerical probability when a named empirical model or base-rate
dataset supports it, with provenance, assumptions, and uncertainty. It is an
additional claim to verify, not a substitute for verification or a default output.

Historical calibration entries remain readable through bin/calibration. Their
legacy HIGH thresholds do not affect this contract. New outcome records can use
UNRATED with null rubric/posterior; no score is needed to follow up on a decision.
