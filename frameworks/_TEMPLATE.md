# Compact method card

Add a catalog entry with a written, observable trigger and a fact key. Keep the
method card below 4 KB. Describe the deciding question, a short procedure, the
artifact, and how findings affect claims. No fixed output counts or confidence
points. Additional depth belongs in references/deep and is read by section on
demand. N/A must quote the catalog trigger; unsupported applicability is BLOCKED.

Every method returns hashed evidence through the same gate contract. A method
cannot replace evidence, independent refutation, or consumer verification.
